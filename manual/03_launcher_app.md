# Chapter 3: Home Screen

> **App Version**: v3.0.0  
> **Firmware Version**: v3.0.0  
> **Last Verified**: 2026-10-26  

---

## Overview
Since v3.0 the whole system is **portrait only** (540 x 960). The home screen shows:

1. **Status strip** (on every screen): Bluetooth state on the left, the time in the middle, battery percentage and icon on the right (lightning bolt while on USB).
2. **Clock**: the date and a large time. Only this area refreshes when the minute changes; the rest of the screen stays still.
3. **Continue reading** card: the last book you had open, with its progress. Tap it to go straight back to your page. With no history yet it opens the library.
4. **Tiles** (2 x 4): Library, Bookmarks, Pictures, Files, Calculator, Games (Chapter 10), Settings and Sleep. The whole tile is a large touch target. Bluetooth state is shown in the status strip and in Settings > Bluetooth.

## Navigation
- Every app screen has a **back chevron** at the top left. It goes up one level inside the app (for example from a book's details back to the list), and from an app's main screen back Home.
- The side button sleeps and wakes the device from anywhere (see Chapter 9).

## For developers
- All screens use the shared kit in `src/ui/UIKit.h` (header, list rows, pager, buttons, toggles, segmented controls, icons).
- Apps are identified by `AppId` in `include/Launcher.h`; launching or leaving an app from inside a touch handler must use the deferred `Launcher::requestLaunch()` / `requestExit()`.
- The back chevron is handled centrally: `Application::wantsSystemBack()` / `onBack()`.
- Icons are generated by `tools/generate_ui_icons.py` into `src/ui/ui_icons.h`.
